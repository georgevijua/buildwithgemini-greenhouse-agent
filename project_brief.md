# My agent: greenhouse-agent
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps plant enthusiasts care for, manage, and discover plants with a catalog of house plants and botanical care guides.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers user experience level, lighting conditions at home, and watering history
- Tools: Look up plant details, query catalog by light/water needs, add new plants, update watering logs
- Catalog/UI: Collection of house plants with care schedules, sunlight needs, and difficulty levels
- Image gen: Visual previews of plant growth, flowering states, and decorative placements
- Sandbox: Watering frequency and humidity calculations based on room metrics

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for environmental care calculations, Cloud Trace
